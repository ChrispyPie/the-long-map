Before you begin

A journey on a map through the history of the Jewish people, told from their perspective, one source at a time.

This is Book One of The Long Map, a series that tells history from the perspective of different peoples, on the same map and with the same rules for sources. Here, other peoples appear as Israel’s neighbours. In their own books, they stand at the centre. The aim is simple: to help us understand one another a little better, and to leave less room for hate.

  • Book One · Story of the Jews You are here
  • Book Two · Story of the Arabs Coming later
  • Story of the Persians Coming later

What this is

  • An interactive retelling of Jewish history, built on sources. You explore it year by year.
  • Every claim has a source and a label: Attested found in ancient evidence, Disputed scholars disagree, Tradition Jewish tradition and the Hebrew Bible, told with respect.
  • Short Imagine lines set the scene and add no facts.
  • Maps and borders are approximate drawings, not exact lines.

What this is not

  • Not a religious text. It does not decide questions of faith.
  • Not political advocacy about modern conflicts.
  • Not the final word. Scholars keep learning. If you find a mistake, check the sources in the fact box and tell us, with a source.
